    summary: "Used to notify the court that you disclaim an inheritance, for example if the deceased’s debts exceed the assets or you do not wish to accept the inheritance for another reason."
    deadline:
      label: "3 months"
      detail: "For legal heirs, the period is 3 months from learning of the death and of being an heir. After that, the inheritance is deemed accepted unconditionally. The disclaimer must be unconditional."
    authority: "The civil court of peace at the deceased’s last place of residence"
    steps:
      - "Fill in the deceased’s name, date of death, last place of residence and your relationship to them."
      - "Attach a civil registry record and the death certificate."
      - "File the petition with the civil court of peace at the deceased’s last residence within 3 months; do not attach any conditions."
      - "Before disclaiming, do not sell or transfer any estate assets; such acts may be treated as acceptance."
