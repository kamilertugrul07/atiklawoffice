faqs:
  - q: "What are the conditions for an uncontested divorce?"
    a: "The marriage must have lasted at least one year, the spouses must apply together or one must accept the case filed by the other, and the judge must hear both spouses in person. Property, maintenance and custody should be set out clearly in the protocol."
  - q: "Can a divorce be resolved through mediation?"
    a: "Only the court can grant a divorce. However, the spouses can reach agreement through mediation on matters they can freely decide, such as division of property or the amount of maintenance. Disputes involving suspected domestic violence are not suitable for mediation."
  - q: "Who is granted custody?"
    a: "The court decides on custody in the best interests of the child, taking into account the child’s age, needs and views together with the circumstances of the parents."
  - q: "When should a property division case be filed?"
    a: "Claims for the division of matrimonial property are subject to a 10-year limitation period from the date the divorce becomes final. They can be brought together with the divorce or as a separate case."

Family law disputes are processes in which the human side weighs as heavily as the legal one. Especially where there are children, the way the parties communicate with each other matters as much as the outcome of the case.

We stand by you in divorce, custody, maintenance and property division with care for confidentiality. We give priority to agreed solutions where the spouses can reach agreement, and defend your rights in court where agreement is not possible.
