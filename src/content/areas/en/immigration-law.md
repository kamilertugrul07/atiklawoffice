For foreigners who wish to live or work in Türkiye or stay with their family, choosing the right type of permit and applying on time is decisive. Applications made after the visa has expired may be refused, and time limits against refusal and deportation orders are very short.

We advise foreign nationals and their family members on residence and work permits, from the application to the issue of the card. We represent them in administrative applications and lawsuits against refusal, cancellation and deportation decisions.
