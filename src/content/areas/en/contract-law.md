faqs:
  - q: "Is an oral agreement valid?"
    a: "Contracts for which the law does not prescribe a specific form can be concluded orally. However, proof becomes difficult in a dispute, so important agreements should be made in writing."
  - q: "What does a mediation clause achieve?"
    a: "The parties can agree in the contract to turn to a mediator first if a dispute arises. This ensures from the outset that the parties sit down at the table before going to court."
  - q: "What can I do if the other side does not perform?"
    a: "Performance is first demanded by written notice; depending on the contract and the law, an action for performance, termination, a contractual penalty or damages may follow. Mediation is mandatory before suing for commercial receivables."
  - q: "When does a claim become time-barred?"
    a: "The general limitation period is 10 years; for periodic claims such as rent, interest and wages it is 5 years. The law sets shorter periods for some types of contract."

A well-written contract resolves a dispute before it arises. When the rights and obligations of the parties, and the path to follow if a problem occurs, are written clearly from the start, the relationship itself is protected too.

We draft contracts for companies and individuals, review drafts received from the other side and stand by you during negotiations. In case of breach, we try the path of agreement first and, if necessary, pursue your claim in court.
