Consumer disputes often seem small in value, yet they can become a loss of time and effort if the wrong path is chosen. Depending on the amount, the arbitration committee, mediation or the consumer court should be used.

We advise consumers on defective goods and services, distance sales and housing disputes, and sellers and providers on compliance and complaint handling.
