There is more than one route to Turkish citizenship, and each has its own conditions, documents and timetable. In investment applications the value and title of the property are decisive; in applications by marriage, the continuation of family unity.

We advise foreign investors and their families from the legal review of the investment to the preparation and conclusion of the application. We can also hold meetings in English or Russian.
