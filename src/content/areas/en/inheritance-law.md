  - q: "How much time do I have to disclaim an inheritance?"
    a: "An inheritance must be disclaimed before the civil court of peace within 3 months of learning of the death and of being an heir. After this period the inheritance is deemed accepted."
  - q: "Is mediation required before an action to end co-ownership?"
    a: "Yes. For disputes on the partition of immovable property and ending co-ownership, applying to a mediator before filing suit is mandatory. This is an opportunity for the heirs to agree on a division instead of a sale."

Inheritance comes up at one of the most fragile moments for family ties. Short time limits, heirs living abroad and properties that cannot be divided can make the process difficult both legally and emotionally.

We stand by heirs from obtaining the certificate of inheritance to the distribution of the estate. In distribution disputes our aim is first for the heirs to meet in a common solution and, where this is not possible, to protect their rights in court.
