  - q: "Can a foreign arbitral award be enforced in Türkiye?"

When a dispute arises in cross-border trade, which country’s law applies and where the decision can be enforced become as important as the dispute itself. These questions are often answered on the day the contract is first written.

We advise on commercial relationships with a foreign element, from drafting contracts to arbitration and enforcement. We also value helping parties in different countries reach a common solution through online mediation.
