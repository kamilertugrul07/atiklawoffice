faqs:
  - q: "Can I be dismissed for joining a union?"
    a: "No. An employment contract cannot be terminated because of union membership or participation in union activities. In such a dismissal, the employee may claim union compensation of at least one year’s wages in addition to reinstatement."
  - q: "How do I become a union member?"
    a: "Joining and leaving a union is done through e-Devlet (e-Government). It is unlawful for the employer to ask about membership or try to prevent it."
  - q: "How can a competence determination be challenged?"
    a: "The Ministry’s competence determination can be challenged before the labour court within 6 working days of notification. The collective bargaining process is suspended during the case."
  - q: "What happens if no agreement is reached in collective bargaining?"
    a: "Upon the application of either party, the dispute is referred to an official mediator. If no agreement is reached in mediation either, strike and lock-out may be resorted to under the conditions set by law."

Union rights are guaranteed by the Constitution, but exercising them is subject to short time limits and strict formal rules. There are only six working days to object to a competence determination, and every stage of collective bargaining follows a statutory timetable.

We advise and represent employees, unions and employers from membership to collective agreements. In collective labour disputes we believe in the importance of mediation for the parties to find common ground.
