  - q: "Should I set up a limited or a joint-stock company?"
    a: "The number of shareholders, capital, ease of share transfer and management structure are decisive. Minimum capital is TRY 50,000 for a limited company and TRY 250,000 for a joint-stock company; share transfers are more flexible in a joint-stock company."
  - q: "How long does company formation take?"

  - q: "How can a general meeting resolution be challenged?"
    a: "Resolutions contrary to the law, the articles or good faith can be challenged by an action for annulment within three months of the resolution. It is important to vote against the resolution and have this recorded in the minutes."

When a company’s legal structure is set up correctly from day one, many future disputes are avoided. The type of company chosen, the provisions of the articles of association and the agreements between shareholders determine how the company will be run for years to come.

We advise companies from formation to capital changes, share transfers and general meetings. When a dispute arises between shareholders, we first look for a solution at the table and, where necessary, represent our clients in court.
