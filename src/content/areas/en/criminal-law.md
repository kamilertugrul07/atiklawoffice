In criminal proceedings, the first hours and the first statement can determine the course of the file. Whether you face an accusation or have been harmed by an offence, knowing your rights from the outset and having a lawyer at your side at every stage matters.

We act as defence counsel for suspects and defendants and as counsel for victims during investigation and trial. For offences subject to reconciliation, we also take the reconciliation process into account so that the parties can reach a mutual solution.
