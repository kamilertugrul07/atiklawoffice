In disputes between individuals and the administration, time limits are short and usually begin with the notification. A zoning decision, the cancellation of a permit or a disciplinary penalty can become final if not challenged in time.

We stand by individuals and companies against acts and actions of the administration: we review the act and its basis, apply to the administration and administrative courts without missing time limits, and file full-remedy actions to recover your loss.
