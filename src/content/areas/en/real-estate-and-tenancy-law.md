Real estate is often the savings of a lifetime. A legal review before buying, or the right clause added to a lease, can prevent a dispute that might last for years.

We carry out title and zoning reviews before purchases and stand by both tenants and landlords in lease relationships. In tenancy disputes, we see the mandatory pre-trial mediation as an opportunity for a solution at the table.
